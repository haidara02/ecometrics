Week 1:
During the first week, we assigned roles and formed teams for the backend development. Additionally, we worked on designing the database schema and initiated the setup of frontend code.

Week 2:
In the second week, our focus was on researching Firebase and integrating it into our project. I imported sample data into Firestore and created system architecture diagrams. Furthermore, I incorporated theme colors from the Figma design into the Material-UI framework.

Week 3:
Week three involved implementing user context within the application and developing frontend functionalities for login, registration, and email verification. I also performed rigorous error checking, particularly for registration fields like passwords, and introduced an error snackbar alert within the app context. Additionally, I added animations to enhance the user experience on the login and registration pages, which were refactored into a single component.

Week 4:
During the fourth week, I contributed to the development of the companies page by integrating a simple datagrid and an ESG rating bar to get Khoi started. We resolved issues related to the DataGrid component and refactored database filters while introducing additional functions.

Week 5:
In week five, the focus shifted towards a complete overhaul of the Firestore database, including restructuring types and functions.

Week 6:
By the sixth week, we successfully completed the restructuring of the entire database, including types and functions. Additionally, I implemented CSV upload functionality, ensuring parsed data aligned with the appropriate database structure in Firestore and to maximise upload efficiency.

Week 7:
Week seven involved troubleshooting issues with data uploading functions into Firestore in collaboration with Sophie. I also introduced Prettier for code formatting and enhanced styling and functionalities for the user page.

Week 8:
During the eighth week, I improved error messaging for login and user pages, optimized upload functions to handle larger files and repeated uploads, and added functionality to display each company's average ESG scores for recent years. Additionally, I modified the Firestore database structure to enhance efficiency in storing total metric values for each year.

Week 9:
In week nine, I worked on displaying only relevant metrics available to companies on the metrics dashboard. This involved ensuring that the framework metrics search box communicated which metrics were unavailable for selected companies but present in the chosen framework, in collaboration with Sophie.

Week 10:
During the final week, I completed the data upload process and addressed various interface improvements, such as error handling for input fields when editing user details and fixing bugs related to search selectors. Additionally, I added a headquarter country column to the metrics dashboard and resolved issues with the user profile loading icon and search selector functionality.
